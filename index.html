How to read this guide +

This is a research snapshot, not live monitoring. Original assessment: 3 August 2026. Post-assessment validation: 30 September 2026. Check current registry documentation before making operational decisions.

15 ecosystems, 16 profiles. Arch’s official repositories and AUR are separate because their security models are very different. Their CLI ratings follow the report’s shared pacman row; AUR itself uses external recipe/build workflows.

Availability is not adoption. A control may be opt-in, client-version-dependent, or limited to selected packages. Planned features are not counted as shipped. No overall safety score is calculated.

Different architectures need different controls. Go has no registry publishing accounts. CPAN is in the report’s curated cohort, but PAUSE uploads are self-service. Neither classification proves human review or safe code.

Evidence has limits. Inferred or thinly documented cells are marked “Lower-confidence evidence” in expanded details. The report flags selected domain/credential checks, NuGet provenance, Maven scanning, and Debian/Arch authentication practices.

What is an insight? Report findings, derived calculations, and our design implications are labeled separately. Funding patterns are associations, not proof of causation.
